M1 to M3, mark.cr

Marking

mark.cr builds 2M nodes and times forced collections. The profile is M2, where each node points to a String and a 64-byte buffer, with 4 markers and collections back to back: the heap that prefetch on push (843bb7e6) is meant to help. In Compare, pick fix against fix-pf or acton against acton-nopf and watch the share of GC_mark_from.

Result: every variant marks within the run-to-run range of fix. Only stock with 6,500 parked fibers stands out, 4.4 times slower, from the mark stack overflow.

A1 and AX, alloc.cr

Allocation from 4 threads

alloc.cr runs 4 threads in a parallel execution context, each allocating a 32-byte object and a String per step and keeping the latest 200,000. Profiles of fix, fix-fl, acton and acton-noatomicfl. In fix the kernel's futex wake path under GC_generic_malloc_many and GC_release_mark_lock takes a third of the samples; filter on Locks or Kernel to see it, and Compare it with fix-fl.

Result: the 2-line unlock order backport takes 4 threads from 667 to 205 ns per step, the fork to 173. Upstream master is as slow as 8.2.12.

B1, lavinmqperf throughput

Broker at full speed

2 publishers and 2 consumers without a rate limit, 16-byte messages, about 1M msg/s, 30 s profiles of fix and acton at 99 Hz with frame pointers. Filter on GC to see how little of the broker's time is in libgc, and on Allocation for where the message path allocates.

Result: libgc is in 4.4% of the samples with fix and 3.1% with acton. GC time drops by a third, throughput moves within noise.

B2, driver.cr none

Idle broker, 2,000 connections

20,000 queues and 2,000 idle connections, a collection forced every 3 s through /api/nodes/gc_collect. 20 s recordings of stock, fix and acton, starting 20 s into the window. Every parked fiber's stack is a root, which is what overflows 8.2.12's 4,096-entry mark stack. The GC lane and the red stop-the-world bands show each collection's length.

Result: stock pauses about 126 ms on every collection; fix and acton about 31 ms.

B3 and B3U, driver.cr churn

Full load

20,000 flapping queues, 2,000 connections with 200 reconnects per second, and lavinmqperf at 20,000 msg/s. 20 s recordings of stock, fix and acton, 60 s into the window: fiber runs per thread, collections, resident memory against the GC heap, and allocation samples by stack.

Result: pause p50 144 ms on stock, 40 ms on fix and acton. acton holds about 100 MB more resident memory, also with GC_UNMAP_THRESHOLD=1 (B3U).
